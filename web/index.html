        What Hexcrow adds to Ootle’s hash lock and time lock

        An invoice book an accountant can check
        Each invoice is a salted hash in a public book with no owner. Whoever is shown the text recomputes the hash and finds the record, its deadlines and what happened to it.
        A key per invoice for every party
        Client, contractor, mediator and view key are new for each invoice, so two invoices between the same people share nothing on chain.
        A check before the work starts
        The contractor confirms from public data that the escrow exists, holds the agreed amount and is locked by exactly these rules, with no key that could take it back early.
        A mediator for disputes
        It can pay either side, only together with that side’s key, and only until the claim window closes. Its ruling stays off chain; the book keeps its hash.
